# Literature Prioritization

## Objective

After mapping the Trustworthy Agentic AI literature across seven dimensions, the next step was to reduce the initial **21-paper shortlist** to the papers most valuable for deeper study.

## Selection Criteria

Each paper was considered using the same criteria:

- Relevance to Trustworthy Agentic AI
- Novelty potential
- Practical contribution
- Experimental strength
- Research-gap value

The goal was not to deep-read every paper, but to identify the smallest set that provides the strongest understanding of the research landscape.

## Seven Literature Dimensions

1. Explainability & Auditability
2. Uncertainty Quantification & Calibration
3. Reliability & Robustness
4. Safety, Verification & Constraint Satisfaction
5. Multi-Agent Coordination & Trust
6. Security, Privacy & Tool/Environment Safety
7. Evaluation, Monitoring & Assurance

## High-Priority Candidates

The initial ranking produced approximately eight papers for potential deeper study:

- A Unified Framework for the Evaluation of LLM Agentic Capabilities
- Uncertainty Quantification in LLM Agents
- The Verifier Tax
- ReliabilityBench
- Auditable Agents
- Towards Verifiably Safe Tool Use for LLM Agents
- Explicit Trait Inference for Multi-Agent Coordination
- Agent Security Bench (ASB)

## Practical Decision

Because of the limited project timeline, all high-priority papers will **not** be deep-read immediately.

The strategy is:

`Literature landscape → Rapid screening → Identify strongest papers → Select only direction-relevant papers for deep study`

This avoids spending substantial time on papers that may become irrelevant after the final research direction is selected.

## Current Status

The literature mapping has provided enough coverage to identify:

- Major Trustworthy Agentic AI dimensions
- Important active research areas
- Repeated methodological patterns
- Existing limitations
- Potential research opportunities

The next stage is to use this landscape to select a **specific, experimentally testable research direction** rather than continuing broad literature review.
